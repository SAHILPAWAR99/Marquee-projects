2 min Maggie recipie (By Sahil Pawar)

Ingredients

  • 1 packet of Maggie noodles
  • 1 packet of tastemaker masala
  • vegetables for added nutrition(not compulsary)

Instructions

  1. Buy Maggie noodles and tastemaker masala.
  2. Boil 2 cups of water in a pot.
  3. Add the Maggie noodles and the tastemaker masala to the boiling water.
  4. Cook for 2 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the noodles are soft and the masala is well mixed.
  5. Serve hot and enjoy your delicious Maggie!

tip: Add some vegetables like carrots, beans, or spinach for extra nutrition!
