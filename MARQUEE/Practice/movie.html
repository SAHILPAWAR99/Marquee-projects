INTERSTELLAR - Christopher Nolan

Cast Name

  • Matthew McConaughey as Cooper
  • Anne Hathaway as Amelia Brand
  • Jessica Chastain as Murph
  • Michael Caine as Professor Brand

Plot Summary

In the near future, Earth is facing a global food crisis and environmental collapse. A former NASA pilot, Cooper, is recruited for a mission to find a new habitable planet for humanity. Along with a team of astronauts, he travels through a wormhole near Saturn to explore three potential planets orbiting a supermassive black hole called Gargantua. As they navigate the challenges of space travel and time dilation, Cooper must also confront the emotional toll of leaving his family behind on Earth.
